Overview

I am a Graduate Research Assistant at Universiti Malaya, working on lightweight deep learning models for real-time object detection on embedded and edge devices. My work focuses on improving the speed–accuracy trade-off of YOLO-based models for practical deployment in UAV, medical, and industrial applications.

My current research interests include edge AI, efficient computer vision, model optimization, lightweight object detection, and embedded deployment using platforms such as Jetson Nano.
