Brief Bio: Md. Minhazul Haq completed his Master of Engineering Science (by Research) at Universiti Malaya, Malaysia, with the degree awarded by the University Senate on 22 July 2026. His research interests include Embedded AI, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Biomedical Signal Processing, Intelligent Systems, and Autonomous Systems. His master's dissertation, titled "Optimization of Lightweight YOLO Model for Multiclass Object Detection on Embedded Device," focused on developing efficient deep learning models for real-time object detection on resource-constrained embedded platforms.”.

Minhazul was appointed as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Electrical Engineering, Universiti Malaya, under the Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA) Scheme (Oct. 2025 – Feb. 2026), where he conducted laboratory sessions and guided undergraduate students in electrical and electromagnetic experiments. He received his Bachelor of Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), Bangladesh, in 2022.
