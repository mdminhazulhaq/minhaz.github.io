Brief Bio: Md. Minhazul Haq is currently pursuing a Master of Engineering Science (Research) in Artificial Intelligence at Universiti Malaya, Malaysia, since November 2023. His research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision on edge devices, Autonomous Transportation, Pedestrian Detection, and Internet of Things (IoT).
