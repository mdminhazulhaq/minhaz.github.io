Brief Bio: Md. Minhazul Haq is currently pursuing a Master of Engineering Science (Research) in Artificial Intelligence at Universiti Malaya, Malaysia, since November 2023. He is a recipient of the Universiti Malaya Scholarship Scheme (UMSS) and is serving as a Graduate Research Assistant (Nov. 2023 – Feb. 2026), conducting research in the field of Artificial Intelligence at the Centre of Intelligent Systems for Emerging Technology (CISET) Lab, Universiti Malaya. Minhazul is also appointed as a Graduate Teaching Assistant under the Universiti Malaya Graduate Teaching Assistantship (GTA) Scheme (Oct. 2025 – Feb. 2026). He received his Bachelor of Electrical Engineering from Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), Bangladesh, in 2022.

Minhazul’s research and teaching interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision on edge devices, Autonomous Transportation, Pedestrian Detection, and Internet of Things (IoT). His master’s dissertation focuses on the “Optimization of Lightweight YOLO Models for Automated Object Detection on Embedded Devices”.
